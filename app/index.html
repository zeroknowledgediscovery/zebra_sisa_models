The request body below is what gets sent. Edits from the other two tabs land here so you can review them first. Python-style single quotes are tolerated and converted.

Diagnoses ICD-10 / ICD-9

Prescriptions NDC (11-digit)

Procedures CPT / HCPCS / ICD

Bulk paste codes and dates

One entry per line: TYPE, date, code where TYPE is DX, RX or PROC. Dates may be MM-DD-YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YYYY. Lines without a TYPE are treated as DX.

The description is sent to OpenAI from your browser. Codes are generated by the LLM; review them in the JSON tab before relying on the result. Do not enter identifiable patient information.
